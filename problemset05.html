Twelve problems on Lectures 15 and 16: the electrochemically active surface area, the Tafel slope and the step it names, the rotating disk and the ring around it. Eight of them are figures. Part A asks you to read a number off a plot — a capacitance slope, the area of a stripping peak, a Tafel slope, the gap an uncompensated resistance opens, a Levich slope, a Koutecký–Levich intercept, a disk plateau and the ring current beside it — and then use it. Part B shows you a result and asks what it is allowed to mean. Every figure is generated from your student ID, so no two sets are the same: a number copied from anyone else will simply be wrong.
