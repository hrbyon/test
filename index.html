How the concept checks work

Enter your student ID before you begin. That is how your submission is identified — there is no separate account or password.
You get one attempt. Your student ID locks as soon as you answer the first question, and each answer is final.
Every question shows the correct answer and why it is correct the moment you choose — including when you get it wrong. Read those explanations; they are the point of the exercise.
At the end, click Submit to the course to send your result. The download button beside it is a backup, not a second step.
These checks are for learning, not for ranking. They are graded on completion, and the results tell the teaching team which ideas the class has not landed yet — those become the opening of the next lecture. Answer honestly; a wrong answer you understand afterwards is worth more here than a right one you guessed.

Using AI in this course

Use AI to explain a concept, check your reasoning, or generate extra practice. Do not submit AI-written text as your own, and do not use it during closed-book examinations.

The test is simple: you must be able to reproduce and defend every answer you submit, with the AI closed.
