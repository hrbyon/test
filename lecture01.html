How to read the table

The table lists reduction reactions only. To use one as an oxidation, reverse the arrow and flip the sign of E°. A large E° marks a strong oxidizing agent (easily reduced); a small one, a strong reducing agent.

What current efficiency means

In a real cell some of the charge you pass leaks into side reactions such as hydrogen evolution. Drop the efficiency below 100 % to see why a measured mass falls short of the calculated one.
